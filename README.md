# Zachary Gunderson

Edge AI engineer working on Physical AI safety. Monitoring methods and evaluations for robots and agents.

Developing monitoring methods and evaluations that mitigate the physical risks caused by robotic foundation models.

## Projects

- **[Seen vs. Followed](https://i-spy-robot.github.io/seen-vs-followed/)** ([code](https://github.com/I-Spy-Robot/seen-vs-followed)). Do vision-language-action models act on text in the scene? Preliminary results on a DROID sorting task in Isaac Sim.
- **[Stance drift in multi-turn agent conversations](https://ztgunderson.github.io/stance-drift/)** ([code](https://github.com/Ztgunderson/stance-drift)). An AI math tutor that must not give away answers, a student whose tone changes, and what a monitor can see. Continues my [Apart Research project](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/examining-stance-drifting-in-multiturn-agent-interactions-mexs).
- **[I Spy Agent](https://ztgunderson.github.io/I-Spy-Agent/)** ([code](https://github.com/Ztgunderson/I-Spy-Agent)). Small decision models as black-box monitors for covert agent behaviour: sabotage hidden in files (LinuxArena) and collusion through covert table talk (NARCBench-Stego). Extends my [Apart Research sprint report](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/small-decision-models-as-blackbox-monitors-for-covert-agent-behaviour-lrfp).

More: [Sirens for Agents](https://ztgunderson.github.io/sirens_discovery/) (honeypot discovery for AI control, continues an [Apart Research project](https://apartresearch.com/sprints/projects/discovering-honeypots-from-resampled-behavior-in-evaluation-w9u3)), [mirage-probes-steering](https://ztgunderson.github.io/mirage-probes-steering/) (probes and steering vectors on medical VQA), and [Tibetan speech recognition](https://github.com/Ztgunderson/TibetianBuddhismTransciptionPipeline).
